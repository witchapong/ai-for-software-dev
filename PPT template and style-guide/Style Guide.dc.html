03 · Frame and grid
content area — 1744 × 812
Margins are 88px left and right, 56px top, 48px bottom. The title block plus its 1px rule takes the top ~170px; the footer line takes ~48px. Everything between is yours.
That leaves 1744 × 812 for content — roughly 15% more than a stock PowerPoint layout, bought by shrinking the title zone rather than the margins.

05 · Rules
Do
One idea per slide; if a slide needs a second title, it is two slides.
Write titles as topic phrases or plain statements — pick one style per deck and keep it.
Leave the bottom third empty when the content ends there. Air is part of the layout.
Number sections and refer to them by number while speaking.
Cite figures and data on the slide, in mono, at the foot.
Don't
No gradients, shadows, rounded cards, or coloured callout boxes.
No emoji, no clip art, no icon decoration that isn't carrying information.
No text over a photograph; use the caption band.
No third background colour, no second accent hue.
No paragraph on a slide. If it must be read, it belongs in the handout.
