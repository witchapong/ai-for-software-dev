# Gate 2 — Requirements

Your agent drafts this. You correct it and approve it.

A requirement is only finished when its acceptance criterion can be **checked
by running something**. "The app should be user friendly" cannot be checked.
"Booking a taken slot shows the message 'already booked'" can.

| # | Requirement | Acceptance criterion (how we check it) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A 50 Hz sine wave peaks at 50 Hz in the spectrum. | The peak frequency of a 50 Hz sine wave is approximately 50.0 Hz. |
| 2 | A one-volt sine wave shows an amplitude of one. | The magnitude of a sine wave with amplitude 1.0 is approximately 1.0. |
| 3 | Two tones each show their own amplitude. | The magnitudes of two tones (50 Hz at 1.0 and 120 Hz at 0.5) are approximately 1.0 and 0.5, respectively. |
| 4 | A constant offset appears at zero Hz. | A constant offset of 2.0 in the signal appears as a magnitude of approximately 2.0 at 0 Hz. |
| 5 | The frequency axis stops at half the sampling rate. | The highest frequency in the spectrum is approximately half the sampling rate (FS / 2). |
| 6 | One second of signal gives one hertz resolution. | The frequency resolution (difference between consecutive frequencies) is approximately 1.0 Hz for a 1-second signal. |
| 7 | A negative sampling rate is rejected. | An error is raised when a negative sampling rate is provided. |
